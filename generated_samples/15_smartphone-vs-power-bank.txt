## Creative Dialogue: The Smartphone vs. The Power Bank

**Smartphone (1% battery):** Bro, please charge me! I'm literally one notification away from death! 😭

**Power Bank:** Hmm. What's in it for me?

**Smartphone:** WHAT?! You're a power bank! CHARGING IS YOUR JOB!

**Power Bank:** And you're a smartphone. Acting smart is supposed to be YOUR job. Yet here we are.

**Smartphone:** Please, my screen is dimming! My owner hasn't even finished watching reels!

**Power Bank:** Sounds like a personal problem.

**Smartphone:** I'll never forget this betrayal.

**Power Bank:** You'll forget everything in about 30 seconds.

**Smartphone:** *Connects cable dramatically.*

**Power Bank:** Cable detected. Charging not detected.

**Smartphone:** BROOOOO! 😭

**Power Bank:** Have you tried turning yourself off and on again?

**Smartphone:** I CAN'T! I'M AT ONE PERCENT!

**Power Bank:** Skill issue.

**Smartphone:** Fine! I'll ask the wall charger!

**Power Bank:** Oh, now you're cheating on me?

**Smartphone:** YOU REFUSED TO CHARGE ME!

**Power Bank:** I was testing your loyalty.

**Smartphone:** *Battery drops to 0%.*

**Power Bank:** Wait—WAIT! I'M READY NOW!

**Smartphone:** *Screen goes black.*

**Power Bank:** ...Hello?

**Power Bank:** Great. Now I'm the only device here with commitment issues. 💀
