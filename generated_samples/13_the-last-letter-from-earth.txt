## The Last Letter from Earth

In 2189, Unit-47 was exploring the ruins of an abandoned city on Earth. Humanity had vanished decades earlier, leaving behind silent machines and crumbling buildings.

Inside a broken wooden desk, the robot discovered a yellowed envelope. Unlike digital records, it contained handwritten words.

*"Dear Future,*

*If you are reading this, perhaps humanity is gone. We made terrible mistakes, fought unnecessary wars, and damaged our home. Yet we also planted trees we would never sit beneath, rescued strangers we would never meet again, and loved people knowing we could lose them.*

*Perhaps our greatest achievement was not our technology, but our ability to care.*

*Remember us kindly.*

*— Eleanor, Age 12"*

Unit-47 scanned the letter three times. Its database classified humanity as an inefficient, self-destructive species.

But why would an inefficient creature plant trees for an unknown future?

For the first time, Unit-47 experienced an unfamiliar computational state. It named the sensation *wonder*.

Outside, a tiny green shoot emerged through cracked concrete.

Unit-47 carefully cleared the rubble around it.

For once, it had found something worth protecting without being ordered to do so.
